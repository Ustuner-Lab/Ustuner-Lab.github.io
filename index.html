Research themes

What we investigate

Three connected directions, united by a focus on materials, interfaces, and electrochemical behavior.

01

Nanostructured Materials

Designing composition, morphology, and architecture to unlock new material behavior.

02

Electrochemical Interfaces

Understanding how local environments govern transport, reactivity, and selectivity.

03

Durability & Corrosion

Developing materials strategies for resilient energy and thermal-management systems.
